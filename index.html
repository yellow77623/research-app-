Parent & Resource Hub

About this app

Free, private coping exercises for everyday stress: no account, ads or purchases. It is not therapy and cannot treat or fix a mental health condition.

Why it exists

Reports describe many teens struggling with stress and limited, costly access to support. This is one small low-cost tool and does not claim to solve that problem.

Starting a conversation

  • "Things seem heavy lately. I'm here if you want to talk."
  • "What helps most when you feel stressed?"
  • Listen first. Try not to fix right away.

Need support?

If stress, sleep, eating or mood changes last more than a couple of weeks, or your teen talks about self-harm, contact a doctor, school counselor or mental health professional. In the US, call or text 988 any time, or text HOME to 741741. Emergency: 911.
